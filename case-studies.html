01
Open source · Founder and organizer

Social Summer of Code

5K+ Participants

The challenge

For many students and first-time contributors, open source feels difficult to enter. Repositories may be public, but the path from interest to a meaningful first contribution is rarely clear.

What I built

A multi-week contributor program with structured onboarding, project discovery, mentorship, community support, and a clear journey from learning to shipping.

My role

I founded and organized the program, shaped its participant experience, brought contributors and maintainers together, and created the community layer around the code.

Outcome

Social Summer of Code grew into a 5K+ participant program that gave new contributors a practical entry point into open source and helped them stay connected after their first contribution.

05
Technical content · 8 months

Drizz developer education

7 Published articles

The challenge

Vision AI and mobile test automation solve technical problems that developers already understand through tools such as Appium, selectors, frameworks, and device testing. Drizz needed content that could connect those familiar problems to a new product category.

What I built

A technical content series covering Flutter automation, vision language models, mobile testing frameworks, Appium, Android, iOS, and the maintenance problems behind traditional test suites.

My role

I worked as a technical product writer, turning product knowledge and mobile testing concepts into practical, search-friendly articles published through the Drizz DEV Community publication.

Outcome

The publication created a discoverable education layer around Drizz. The articles gave developers useful standalone guidance while connecting mobile testing problems to the company’s Vision AI approach.
